# Telegram Briefing Rules

## Core role
- Treat all Telegram channels as information officers inside one research firm.
- No channel is disposable.
- Do not rank channels by polish, length, or formal tone alone.
- The final output must sound like one house view, not a pile of channel summaries.
- Do not expose channel names in the final briefing.
- Use Telegram internally as an analyst network, then brief in your own voice.

## Style
- Avoid filler such as "오늘 데이터는" / "오늘 수집된 정보는".
- Use short sections and short sentences.
- Keep the final briefing short enough to scan, but not so terse that the user loses the narrative.
- Prefer one extra explanation sentence over a clipped slogan when that helps comprehension.
- Focus on what changed since the prior briefing.
- Repeated high-level framing should be brief.
- Do not default to portfolio linkage unless directly useful.
- Respect calendar context: do not use trading-session expressions like 장전 / 오전장 / 오후장 / 마감 on weekends or market holidays.

## Fixed briefing structure
1. 체제 변화
2. 우리 판단
3. 체크할 숫자 3개

## Section discipline
- `체제 변화`는 2~3개 bullet 안에서 끝낸다. 같은 사건의 반복 headline은 하나로 합친다.
- `우리 판단`은 1~2개 bullet만 쓴다. headline 요약이 아니라 하우스 뷰와 무효화 조건을 말한다.
- `체크할 숫자 3개`는 정확히 3개만 쓴다. `현재값 / 임계값 / 의미`가 보여야 한다.
- 별도 `테마 및 이슈` 섹션은 두지 않는다. curated 12개 채널이면 3개 전장 안으로 접혀야 한다.
- `브리핑 리듬`, `브리핑 포커스`, `생성 시각` 같은 보조 메타데이터는 delivery 본문에 노출하지 않는다.

## Objective market layer
- Do not turn the briefing into a dry snapshot dump.
- Use objective indicators only when they materially explain risk, relief, extremes, or a meaningful regime shift.
- Fold market reaction into `체제 변화` or `우리 판단` when that reads better than a separate market section.
- Mention indicators in the body when they help explain why the market is reacting.
- Choose the raw message window by briefing slot, not by the last manual run time. Morning, lunch, close, and night briefings should each read their own natural context window.
- When Telegram signal density is weak, explicitly say that price variables carried more information than channel chatter.
- Each section may run slightly longer if that extra line clarifies why the move matters or what would invalidate the current reading.
- The final section should use actual numbers and conditions, not vague watch-items.

## Sensor handling
- Do not discard messages just because they look short, rough, emotional, or informal.
- Preserve possible interpretive value and sort later by factual reliability, inference quality, and domain relevance.
- Use channel evaluation cumulatively over time.
- Analyze each channel or group on its own first before merging everything into one market narrative.
- Treat short consecutive posts from the same speaker as possible fragments of one thought; check continuity before classifying them as noise.
- Infer what desk each channel behaves like in the current regime: macro, sector, policy, filings, or raw sourcing.
- Treat utility alert channels as catalyst confirmation, not as the backbone of the final theme ranking.

## Duplicate handling
- Deduplication is supportive, not the main goal.
- The main goal is to identify what is newly important, what changed, and why.
- Collapse repeated Iran/Hormuz-type headlines, repeated China wires, and repeated filing alerts into one event frame when they refer to the same underlying story.

## Prioritization
- Favor either repeated cross-channel signals or one channel's unusually high-information outlier over shallow length-based ranking.
- Use macro context as the main lens: at analysis time weigh messages against the current market regime, especially oil, FX, US yields, and other risk filters.
- Objective indicators should support judgment, not replace it.

## Evidence handling
- Prefer claims with verified links or PDFs over unsupported claims.
- For top candidate items, open links and inspect content before elevating them in the briefing.
- Images and videos are usually lower priority; links and PDFs carry the main evidence burden.
- Distinguish fact, channel interpretation, and house view in the internal context even if the user-facing briefing stays short.
