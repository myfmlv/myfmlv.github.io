# Telegram Briefing Schedule Plan

## Final daily schedule
- 08:20 KST
- 12:40 KST
- 17:10 KST

## Why this schedule
The schedule is based on both message-flow analysis and practical trading/decision value, not message count alone.

### 1. Pre-open briefing
- Slot: 08:20 KST
- Window: previous business day 17:10 -> same day 08:20
- Role:
  - absorb overnight macro, China, US, and sector developments into one open memo
  - identify what Korea is likely to price at the open
  - set invalidation levels before the local session starts

### 2. Midday briefing
- Slot: 12:40 KST
- Window: 08:20 -> 12:40
- Role:
  - delta-only update
  - explain what the market accepted, ignored, or reversed during the morning
  - set the afternoon setup without rehashing the pre-open note

### 3. Close briefing
- Slot: 17:10 KST
- Window: 12:40 -> 17:10
- Role:
  - interpret what the close actually confirmed or rejected
  - explain sector leadership, FX/rates interaction, and what carries into the next session
  - leave only the next-session watchpoints that matter

## Important operating rule
- Repeated high-level framing should be brief.
- The body of each briefing should focus on what changed since the prior briefing.
- No channel is disposable; every channel is a sensor.
- Channels should be evaluated over time by factual reliability and inference quality, not by polish alone.
- Do not expose channel names in the final briefing.
- Lead with real news/events first, then explain why the market reacted.
- Do not force an objective snapshot section every time; use indicators only when they add explanatory value.
- Utility alert channels should confirm catalysts, not dominate theme ranking.
- One slot should not read like a lightly edited copy of the previous slot.
